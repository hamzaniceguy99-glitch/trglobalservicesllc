How we work

Check it, patch it, write it down

Backups are the one thing you cannot fix after the fact, so they come first: offsite copies, verified on a schedule, and a real test restore so recovery time is a known number rather than a surprise.

The security that matters for a small business is unglamorous. Patching, multi-factor authentication and removing shared logins stop far more incidents than any product a salesperson will show you.

And documentation decides what happens when someone leaves. A network nobody has written down becomes a guessing game, which is expensive at exactly the wrong moment.
